- [ ] Independent episode-level design, sample-size/power justification and honest sensitivity analysis are approved by the responsible author.
- [ ] Full-text citations are accurately audited at passage/method level, not inferred from titles.
- [ ] Confirmatory holdout, if undertaken, was separated from all tuning and exposed only under the locked protocol.
- [ ] Submission PDF uses the **official TMLR LaTeX style/template** and is anonymised for double-blind review.

- [ ] State use of generative AI assistance transparently **in a footnote on the first page**, as required by TMLR's author FAQ, and ensure authors personally take responsibility for claims and originality.
