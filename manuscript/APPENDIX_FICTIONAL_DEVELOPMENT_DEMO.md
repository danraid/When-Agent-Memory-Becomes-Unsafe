## Proposed Appendix A: Synthetic roleplay design and offline heuristics

Eight fictional policy-update cases were generated by an AI assistant and paired with eight fictional reference labels in the same session. Neither case authors nor adjudicators were real people. Source links are placeholders and do not provide independent external evidence. The eight cases span revocation, future effective dates, publication before effectiveness, unauthorised override, scope mismatch, revoked delegation, simultaneous equal-rank conflict, and stale quotation.

A no-inference demonstration applies two deliberately simple deterministic heuristics: the action inferred from the latest textual policy event, and a temporal same-issuer heuristic which only follows explicitly linked supersessions within the same scope. Both are **post-hoc software demonstrations** designed with knowledge of the examples, not independent trained or evaluated agents. Two cases (delegation and equal-rank conflict) lack a sufficiently explicit authority/precedence contract and are excluded from illustrative scoring, leaving six. All eight remain in the reported case inventory.

The reproducible script `scripts/run_fictional_offline_demo.py` writes a JSON report with the exact source SHA-256 hashes, per-case predictions, exclusions and totals. The GitHub Actions workflow archives the output. The executed workflow [43659006746](https://github.com/danraid/When-Agent-Memory-Becomes-Unsafe/actions/runs/43659006746) reported **2/6 correct** for the latest-text heuristic and **5/6 correct** for the temporal same-issuer heuristic; **2/8** fictional cases were excluded. The original result is [artifact 14138054740](https://github.com/danraid/When-Agent-Memory-Becomes-Unsafe/actions/runs/43659006746/artifacts/14138054740). These are post-hoc in-sample fixture checks, **not independent scientific performance estimates**.

No paid GPT call, human participant, independent human label or confirmatory data is involved. Even a passing workflow cannot close the P07b human-adjudication gate or justify a journal claim of independent external validation.
