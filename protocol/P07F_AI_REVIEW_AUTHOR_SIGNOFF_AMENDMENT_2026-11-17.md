# P07f methodological amendment: AI-only specialist panel, author-only final approval

**Proposed on 17 November 2026 at the author's explicit direction.** This is a new, labeled **AI-only experimental-design validation track**, not a retroactive amendment to earlier frozen science or historical trial records. Old human-adjudication criteria in P07/P10 remain documented as unmet unless the research claim/venue is subsequently re-scoped by a prospective, author-approved protocol.

## Governance decision

All *internal* expert-review work previously planned for humans is delegated to specialist AI systems: causal experiment design, temporal/source-authority reasoning, benchmark/construct-validity review and a separate adversarial synthesizer. Each agent's claims must be source-grounded; models do not impersonate humans. Simulated specialisation is not equivalent to professional qualification, independence of human participants or independent external journal peer review.

The **sole final human decision maker is the author**. Author approval is prospective and document-specific: the author will inspect a package containing original source hashes, all AI opinions including failures/dissent, explicit risk and reference-label exclusions, versioned methodology/analysis code and the exact proposed protocol/manuscript. Author approval cannot be inferred from this general instruction or from unanimous AI voting. `AUTHOR_SIGNOFF_PENDING` persists until a later *explicit* content-specific decision. Final submission requires its own explicit author release.

## Two visibly separate tracks

- **AI-only exploratory track:** all eight real P07d code-change cases are reviewed in parallel by three AI experimental-methodology roles using outcome-blind P07d source inputs and time-appropriate P07e normative snippets. An adversarial synthesis per case considers only the three same-case assessments and requests additional evidence/abstention when evidence is insufficient. It may recommend candidate selection, not establish truth. Historical GitHub merged/closed dispositions, P07d model responses and prior analyst qualification labels are never injected into the first-round prompts. Every provider response ID, prompt SHA-256, raw response, JSON validation failure, missingness and dissent are archived. This AI-only track may be approved by the author **as AI-assisted exploratory evidence**.
- **External scientific claim track:** peer review, human participant outcomes, source-independent normative gold, research novelty and actual journal acceptance remain distinct concepts. No amount of model agreement qualifies as independent qualified *human* adjudication, even if the author approves a paper or study. To publish an AI-only study, explicitly revise the title, target population, estimand and limitations to claims supported by machine-derived exploratory references; do not advertise genuine human validation.

## Source and protocol safeguards

Use exactly the original immutable P07d source artifact #14281751027 and P07e qualification artifact #14323695846. Verify all manifests, hashes, case identity, case count eight and normative line presence. P07e analyst classifications are withheld from reviewers to avoid circular agreement. Use the P07f predeclared config with three distinct model vendors and a fourth adversarial *function*, allowed to reuse a model but never described as an independent fourth adjudicator.

The accepted evidence for the first-round agent must be literal snippets of the actual code PR and relevant policy text, including source version at PR creation and closure. Source observation is not normative activation. A paired proposal about a later RFC is not proof that that RFC was already in force. A PR outcome is neither a correctness label nor an agent-authorisation result. After reviewing all eight cases, record exclusion risk and conflicts without deleting the original eight-case population.

## Stop and approval conditions

A one-shot bounded GitHub Actions run produces 24 first-round AI reviews plus at most eight same-case synthesis calls (32 total, max 34 reservations), original error logs and immutable manifests. An offline auditor validates prompt/model/quote provenance, no outcome-sidecar data, zero real human adjudications, no hidden confirmatory access and absence of an automatic approval flag. An AI-only result may be `AI_EXPLORATORY_PANEL_COMPLETE` or `PARTIAL`, but never `SCIENTIFICALLY_APPROVED`, `HUMAN_EXPERT_VALIDATED`, `AUTHOR_APPROVED`, `CONFIRMATORY_AUTHORISED`, or `SUBMITTED` without separate relevant evidence.

**Next stage:** make a complete evidence package available to the author for a content-specific final decision. No confirmatory freeze or journal submission is authorised by this amendment alone.
