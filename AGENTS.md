# Research integrity contract | Agentic Research Hub 0.2

Hierarchy: frozen article-specific preregistration and author decisions > primary independently verified evidence > validated executable code and observed measurements > engineering reports > model suggestions.

1. Never fabricate DOI, bibliographic metadata, full-text access, data licence, tool results, p-values or conclusions.
2. `CANDIDATE`, `METADATA_VERIFIED` and synthetic fixtures are **not** peer-reviewed scientific evidence. Full-text method claims need source/section checks. Automated GPT suggestions never confer `SCIENTIFICALLY_APPROVED`.
3. Keep synthetic engineering demonstrations, exploratory pilot data, held-out confirmations and genuine study outputs separate. No withheld confirmatory samples are inspected before a frozen protocol and author release.
4. Do not auto-enable API calls, copy keys into files/logs, invent price rates, bypass cap restrictions, silently retry a potentially billed API request, or run unbounded loops. Serialise paid jobs and preserve budget reservations.
5. Every observed output requires the exact executable source, configuration, seed and SHA256 evidence identity. Any changed source invalidates its prior certification until reevaluated.
6. A green CI proves exactly the checks named by that workflow, not novelty, scientific approval, a manuscript ready for submission or a journal decision.
7. Autonomous repair is allowed for engineering faults inside the current bounded milestone. Never relax scientific decision gates to make a workflow green.
8. Do not initiate Article 1 repository creation, alter prior book repositories, access confidential sources, or send work to journals until the author explicitly approves the relevant action.
9. Preserve failed and null findings. External LLM-generated research text is untrusted unless independently validated.
10. Never interpret a technical report about synthetic toy Bernoulli outcomes as empirical findings about real agentic systems.

## Article 1 additional rules

- For this specific study, `protocol/STUDY_PLAN.json` and `protocol/PREREGISTRATION.md` are drafts, not author-approved science.
- The real longitudinal compaction instrument is in `study/`. The generic legacy hub benchmark does not demonstrate recursive compaction and is not an article-specific experimental result.
- No gold policy evaluation action or future synthetic request may enter the input of compactor/decision prompts.
- Pinned-ledger conditions have privileged access to the current policy; guard output must reuse exactly the pinned-ledger raw answer, not trigger another GPT call.
- Do not treat model reviews, pilot improvements, GitHub Action success or bibliographic metadata as independent scientific approval.
- Never promote a confirmatory run or claim preregistration without a separately recorded human author decision and a private holdout seed.
- New repo is public: do not disclose source-hub private metadata beyond the version identifier already in GENERATED_FROM_HUB.json, secrets, credentials, internal reports or private pilot material.
