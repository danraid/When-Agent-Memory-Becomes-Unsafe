# Publication pathway decision: negative-results / measurement paper

**Date:** 14 November 2026. **Status:** AUTHORISED EDITORIAL REFRAMING; NOT SCIENTIFICALLY ACCEPTED OR SUBMITTED.

## Choice and rationale

The eight-episode and four-episode GPT pilots show zero observed advantage of version-aware ledgers over recursive summaries on the primary candidate contrasts. In the four-episode temporal study, every evaluated method correctly handled all explicit revocation and new-policy probes. The meaningful contribution that can currently be stated is a *measurement and negative-results observation*: synthetic temporal-policy probes can saturate and fail to discriminate memory systems, even when a recent-window baseline fails on other controls. A separate source-faithful component audit shows finite-budget infeasibility in TypeCompact, which is already an upstream feature, not a new algorithm.

**Reject** a positive claim that ledgers outperform Knowledge Triage or that temporal supersession has been solved. **Reject** pooling the two development instruments, pseudoreplicating turns, treating the adapted typed archive as the full original method, or claiming significance/equivalence from zeros.

**Choose** a candidate *exploratory methodological/negative-results article*, with the contribution limited to: (1) provenance-audited description of two saturated synthetic instruments, (2) explicit error-category separation and recognition of a control-versus-policy-probe mismatch, (3) public-source original-component feasibility check, (4) reusable criteria for future discriminative tests. This is a **positioning hypothesis**, not an established novelty or journal acceptance claim.

## Evidence-supported text for editors

> In two small, independently archived development pilots, policy-supersession probes were solved without observed errors by all tested methods, yielding no evidence that a version-aware ledger improves upon recursive summarisation in this setting. The result is a ceiling-effect warning about the experimental instrument, not a demonstrated safety improvement. A separate pinned upstream TypeCompact component audit identifies infeasible compression budgets on the public sample. Full framework comparison, independently adjudicated naturalistic cases and prospective confirmation are outstanding.

## Discriminative extension (separate future research)

Design a *new* development-only instrument with genuine multi-authority conflicts, ambiguous temporal precedence, delayed publication versus effective dates, revocation scope and independently labelled decision gold. Match the information accessible to the summariser, ledger and full Knowledge Triage framework. Pilot on a bounded sample and assess whether there is a non-saturated distribution before author-locking a confirmatory protocol. Do not retroactively re-label v2/v3 or touch the private confirmatory set.
