1. [Submission instructions](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/submissions.html): upload a PDF prepared with the official mandatory LaTeX style and template; submit through OpenReview; follow dual-submission and ethical policies.

4. [FAQ](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/faq.html): AI-assisted writing is permitted with explicit disclosure in a first-page footnote; authors remain responsible for ideas, claims and originality.

## Submission positioning

The active paper is an exploratory **negative-results / evaluation-instrument diagnosis**, not a new agent-memory algorithm, independently replicated framework, human-adjudicated policy benchmark or confirmed positive intervention. The original pilots (8 and 4 episodes) and original TypeCompact operator sweep are the empirical core. The 16 AI-authored v2 scenarios are post-hoc supplementary development examples only. Two simulated AI review roles are not independent human adjudicators. The full TypeCompact framework was **not** reproduced.

The manuscript may be considered by TMLR only if the editor and reviewers judge its limited empirical contribution sufficient. We cannot guarantee desk-review eligibility or acceptance.
