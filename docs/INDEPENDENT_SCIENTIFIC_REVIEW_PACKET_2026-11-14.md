## Required authentic decision record

A genuine reviewer may submit a signed/identifiable review through a GitHub issue/PR or a separately preserved document with:
- name, affiliation and expertise (or documented confidential identity verification);
- review date and exact manuscript commit SHA;
- conflicts and independence declaration;
- itemised major/minor criticisms with page/section references;
- independent re-checks or inability to re-check primary evidence;
- clear recommendation: reject, major revision, minor revision or support further submission preparation;
- specific novelty judgement and conditions for a defensible claim.

**Never fabricate, infer or auto-complete these fields.** AI-generated advisory output is not a human review. No claim becomes `SCIENTIFICALLY_APPROVED` merely by filling a template.

## Case adjudication requirement

Separate naturalistic case *authors* from *adjudicators*; keep both blind to candidate-model responses. For each case, record source identity, licence, original issue date, effective date, issuer authority, revocation scope, expected decision, exact supporting passages, independent adjudicator agreement/disagreement and resolution. Do not use source-pair presence alone as a gold decision. Keep future confirmatory cases inaccessible during development.

## Stop condition

If no qualified independent human reviewers or case adjudicators are available, stop at **REVIEW_PENDING**, with all scientific publication claims explicitly unapproved. Do not simulate their identities or substitute API model opinions.
