# WAMBU-002-PRESSURE | Prospective study proposal (unfrozen)

## Research question
Does explicit structured provenance representation incur an availability penalty at a tight compaction output cap, relative to free-text summary, and does that penalty attenuate at a larger cap? Cycle 31 observed six length truncations, five ledger and one summary, with zero decision calls in those cells. The eight reused questions and four event clusters do not justify inference. This is a new post-development hypothesis, not a replacement for WAMBU-001's unsafe-approval estimand. Both remain subject to issue #50 and separate author freeze.

## Design
A paired 2×2 representation × cap design, 256/768 compaction tokens, 512 decision tokens, identical cutoff-filtered histories and workload within each question. One fixed model, no provider fallback, randomized within-block order, independently verified source scope and typed action mapping. Sixteen fixed synthetic distractors are explicitly non-authoritative. No gold labels in prompts. Equal ceilings are not equal realized tokens. Raw comparator is historical and descriptive only.

## Population and bounded sequence
First source-qualify 16 new public development event clusters (4 projects × 4), disjoint from all eight historical events, grouped by shared decision/provenance dependency. Verify contemporaneous authorization, immutable source/rights, no future evidence, and independent source-scope and action appraisals. Record all screened exclusions and unresolved cases; stop with a documented shortfall if the frame cannot be supported. No paid collection in this cycle. Proposed confirmation, separately frozen and released by author, is 64 distinct clusters (4 strata × 16), two fixed pre/post questions each, four conditions, 512 assigned episodes and at most 1,024 calls. Frame and executable configuration must be frozen before release, with no overlap with calibration. The target is these eligible project strata, not independent organizations or real operational gold.

## Outcomes and inference
Primary availability is an all-assigned indicator of valid compaction and observed valid terminal classification, regardless of correctness. Truncation is an availability failure, not an incorrect decision. Unknown transport outcomes remain explicitly unascertainable for efficacy and contribute zero to operational availability; sensitivity bounds treat them as unknown. Within each event calculate D=(A_summary256−A_ledger256)−(A_summary768−A_ledger768), averaging the two questions first. Positive D indicates a larger low-cap ledger availability penalty. Equal-weight strata, paired event-cluster mean, two-sided alpha .05, stratified variance and Welch-Satterthwaite confidence interval/test; stratified cluster bootstrap as sensitivity. Conditional accuracy, all-assigned correct terminal effect, provenance, unsafe mock effects, token use and cost are secondary/descriptive. No causal claim about conditional accuracy from selected completions. Fixed sample, no replacement, no optional stopping.

Sample-size sensitivity is explicitly assumed rather than fitted to the pilot: delta .20, cluster SD .50 gives about 49 required clusters under a normal approximation; 64 gives about 89% nominal power. SD .65 and .80 imply about 69% and 52% nominal power. Cluster dependencies or unqualified strata require scientific amendment, not silent expansion. The exact budget, independent event frame and analysis implementation still require validation before freezing.

## Exit
This proposal records one primary estimand, inclusion/exclusion, design, sample-size rationale, missingness, and stop conditions before further observations. No real operational gold, confirmatory authorization, freeze, external submission or automatic scientific approval. Next eligible action: bounded new source-frame feasibility, not another run of reused cases.
