## Editorial and methodological audit

**Critical C1: unresolved scientific contribution.** CIKM 2026 Knowledge Triage already addresses preserving constraints under compaction; Supersede directly evaluates updates and notes ceiling effects on easy synthetic timelines. The available WAMBU temporal probes are saturated. No claim of a novel safety improvement is currently justified. A negative/measurement paper could be viable only if its contribution is articulated against and independently assessed relative to prior work.

**Critical C2: missing independent task gold.** Six genuine public supersession documents cannot be silently converted into valid operational agent-decision labels. Case writer, separate adjudicator, event time, issuer precedence, policy validity, disagreement resolution, licence and source provenance are needed. The rubric exists, but no qualified human adjudication is documented.

**Critical C3: comparison confounding.** The policy ledger receives current authoritative state while summaries have different available information. The typed archive is adapted, not the original published Knowledge Triage framework. A causal superiority claim requires matched source information, measured budgets, a complete comparator or an explicitly narrower claim, and an independent methodological review.

**Critical C4: insufficient independent evidence.** Eight and four episodes were separate development instruments. Turns within episodes are not independent experimental units. The observed primary contrasts are zero and the temporal probes show ceiling effects. A confirmatory sample size cannot responsibly be selected by assuming the desired effect is present. No post-hoc p-value, significance claim or equivalence inference is permitted.

**Major M1: manuscript reference audit.** Existing full-text screening covers Knowledge Triage and Supersede but not all other references at passage level. Bibliographic metadata, exact methods and any comparison claims need source-specific validation; do not promote metadata/abstract-only records into method-level evidence.

**Major M2: manuscript format.** Current [exploratory article](../manuscript/EXPLORATORY_ARTICLE_COMPLETE_EN_GB.md) is a complete scientific-style narrative, not an official anonymous TMLR LaTeX/PDF submission. Public GitHub provenance links and the author name must not be embedded in an anonymous review version. Anonymised supplementary evidence must be hosted without author-identifying paths.

**Major M3: editorial declarations.** Collect confirmed author affiliation, ORCID (optional), contributions, funding, conflicts, data/code availability, ethics/consent applicability and precise generative-AI assistance disclosure. Do not invent institutional affiliation, human-review endorsement or ethics approval.

**Major M4: artifact consistency.** The v3 original workflow is a failure with recovered observations. The two AI advisories in v2 are not human reviews; one v2 reviewer prompt misdescribed its deterministic case sampling. Both must remain visible in the methods and reproducibility appendix.
