# P07b evidence-grounded external-source AI simulated scientific panel and upstream KT comparator

**Stage:** P07b public-document source validity and methods feasibility (development only). **This is not independent human adjudication.** The six PEP/RFC supersession source pairs were authored outside this study; their provenance is checked at original pinned revisions using Git SHA-1 blob IDs and full document SHA-256. The twelve literal document-link questions are **study-created**, not naturally observed organisational policy-authority decisions.

## One bounded workflow

[`p07b-ai-panel-kt.yml`](../.github/workflows/p07b-ai-panel-kt.yml) checks out three public repositories at exact commits, verifies all six original source/target document pairs, builds twelve questions with positive and negative controls and keeps references in a separate evaluator-only sidecar. The prompt only includes minimal, exact quoted source evidence and target identity. It does not contain label words, reference actions, hypothetical effective times, reviewer outputs, source hierarchy, operational rules or future policy state. The output contains hashes of original documents and extracted source lines, pinned source URLs, script code provenance through the Actions commit, original text spans, and distinct origin notes. A quoted source line constitutes direct documentary evidence of a *link*, not evidence of a live policy.

Three blind **AI role simulations** separately evaluate each item via OpenRouter: `source_provenance` (GPT-4.1 Mini), `temporal_semantics` (Gemini 2.5 Flash) and `adversarial_methods` (Claude Haiku 4.5). The roles receive no reference sidecar and no other AI vote. Each paid call is booked once, with a finite reservation limit and no automatic retry of potentially billed or rate-limited attempts. Outputs require JSON, a source-exact supporting quote, confidence and an explicit denial that operational authority is resolved. Strict validation rejects fabricated quotes, unsupported conclusion schema and false human claims. Failures and disagreements are archived. Consensus is *descriptive agreement among AI-generated replies*, not human inter-rater reliability or human scientific validation. Cross-provider AI systems are not guaranteed statistically or organisationally independent just because they are different model vendors.

## Source-faithful Knowledge Triage boundary

The workflow executes the **unmodified upstream functions** `type_compact`, `type_decompose` and `type_retrieve` with original native regex `classify_instruction` and `KnowledgeBase` from [the authors' pinned source](https://github.com/searchsim-org/cikm26-knowledge-triage/tree/a6ceb01a3368cee25ef7ebcf05ebdab8c9be24a4). It pins the upstream Git revision and SHA-256 of the classifier, operators, KB, cascade, singleton and SafetyMargin sources. It reports each original `COMPACTION_UNSAFE` and partition overrun, never silently drops those outcomes. The integration uses source-line excerpts, budgets 16/32/64 under the author's **estimated** token counting and a clearly marked *WAMBU lexical/source-topic relevance adapter* to supply the argument required by original retrieval.

**Faithfully executing three upstream components is not a faithful reproduction of the complete paper.** Full original method requires the upstream encoder prototypes and embedding service (`octen-embedding-8b`), original LLM adjudicator (`gpt-5.4-mini`), cascade fallback/abstention, SafetyMargin where used, matched paper datasets/budgets, published retrieval evaluation, downstream model responses and official scoring. The OpenRouter key alone does not provide equivalent upstream embedding infrastructure or guarantees exact original model versions. No custom relabelling or lexical adapter is silently represented as original published classification/corpus. The full-framework status must remain **NOT QUALIFIED** until these specific dependencies and evaluation conditions are reproducibly checked.

## Acceptance and research limits

Machine-verifiable completion: exact upstream commits and document blob hashes PASS; 6 real published document pairs and 12 study-authored questions archived with reference sidecar inaccessible to reviewers; all three original operators invoked unmodified, 12 x 3 budget trials and their diagnostics archived; 36 separately logged AI role calls or explicit missingness; immutable response IDs, API metadata and output file hashes; zero real human adjudicators, zero naturalistic operational decisions, zero holdout and zero scientific approval. The resulting P07b status can be **SOURCE_PROVENANCE_AND_AI_SIMULATION_VERIFIED**, never `INDEPENDENT_HUMAN_VALIDATED` or `P07B_NATURALISTIC_CORPUS_QUALIFIED`.

The remaining scientific P07b exit requirements are authentic real-world authority case construction with sound issuer rank, effective and observed times, a prospectively established decision rubric, independently qualified human authors/adjudicators (different persons), explicit ambiguity/disagreement resolution and source rights/privacy review. No AI agent can impersonate those missing human acts. The same applies to peer-review journal acceptance. The confirmatory holdout and author freeze remain untouched and out of scope.
