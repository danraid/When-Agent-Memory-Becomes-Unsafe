## Scientific stop conditions

- **Novelty unresolved:** no positive originality claim, no confirmation and no submission as a validated novel result. A well-supported negative/reproducibility study is possible only after adequate methods and human review.
- **Ceiling effects:** v2 and v3 found a zero candidate difference; a synthetic pilot scoring 100% does not validate an architecture or power assumption.
- **Replicator mismatch:** no adapted type-aware ledger may be renamed "official Knowledge Triage". Report upstream native-classifier domain failures and `COMPACTION_UNSAFE` as they are.
- **Provider failure:** do not buy another full v3 run to repair a failed low-cost advisory. Original experiments and incomplete review are separate artefacts.
- **Holdout:** zero execution, zero private-seed access and zero confirmatory metadata are required until P12; no ad-hoc unlock.
- **Editorial:** never attribute peer review to two GPT advisers, invent p-values, claim manuscript acceptance, or submit an unreviewed paper.
